Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an installation take?

Installation time varies by vessel size and system complexity. A typical motor yacht installation takes 2-4 weeks. We'll provide a detailed timeline during consultation.

Do you work on sailing yachts?

Yes, we have extensive experience with both sailing and motor yachts. Each presents unique challenges that we've mastered over 13 years.

What's included in the warranty?

Full parts and labor warranty on all installations. We also offer extended warranty and maintenance packages for ongoing peace of mind.

Can you help with certification?

Absolutely. We provide comprehensive support for IMO type approval and class certification, including documentation, testing, and survey preparation.

Where are you based?

We operate globally with mobile installation capabilities. Our base location allows us to serve the Mediterranean, Caribbean, and Northern Europe regions easily.

What's the ROI on your systems?

Clients typically see fuel savings of 10-20% plus significant maintenance cost reductions. The payback period depends on usage but is often under 3 years.
