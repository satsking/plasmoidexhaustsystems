The Vajra

Designed around two continuous flow paths.

The smaller pipework carries inlet air to the engine. The larger pipework carries exhaust away from it. Both remain separate and are shaped to create controlled swirl through the Vajra.

01 — Inner sphere

Sealed.

The innermost sphere is fully enclosed. It carries no flow and remains isolated from both working paths.

02 — Middle cavity

Inlet air path.

Inlet air flows continuously between the inner and middle sphere walls, swirling from the smaller inlet to the smaller outlet.

03 — Outer cavity

Exhaust path.

Exhaust flows continuously between the middle and outer sphere walls, swirling from the larger inlet to the larger outlet.
