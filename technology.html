What Are Plasmoids?

Plasmoids are coherent structures of plasma and magnetic fields — miniature versions of the phenomena that occur naturally in lightning and stellar environments. They contain enormous energy potential that can be harnessed for practical applications.

Our Thunderstorm Generator creates these plasmoids by collapsing cavitation bubbles. First, a vacuum creates the bubbles, then pressure implodes them — a cycle driven directly by your engine's exhaust.

"This isn't a chemical reaction. It's an atomic reaction that converts harmful emissions into oxygen, literally changing the structure of the molecules involved."

How It Works

Four stages from exhaust gas to clean oxygen

The Mechanism

Understanding the thunderstorm generator process

🌀 Vortex Generator (Vajra)

The Vajra — Sanskrit for "thunderbolt" — is the heart of the system. Engine exhaust enters and is induced to rotate rapidly in an anti-clockwise direction.

Simultaneously, water vapor infused with plasmoids enters the chamber, rotating clockwise. The counter-rotation creates maximum interaction between the energized particles and exhaust molecules.

This vortex structure mirrors the dynamics observed in severe thunderstorms — where extreme temperature differentials create powerful rotating updrafts.

⚛️ Atomic Transmutation

As the exhaust gases and plasmoids interact, an atomic reaction occurs — not a chemical reaction. The difference is profound.

Chemical reactions rearrange molecules. Atomic reactions change the fundamental structure of atoms. In this case, hydrogen protons and electrons bind with carbon atoms to form oxygen.

The result: CO₂ and CO (harmful emissions) are converted to O₂ (life-sustaining oxygen). The reaction is self-sustaining once initiated, with energy coming from the plasmoid field.
